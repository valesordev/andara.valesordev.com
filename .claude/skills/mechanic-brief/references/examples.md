# Examples

## Triggers
**Prompt:** "Cosmology says nomic strain builds up when you use the field. What does that become as a mechanic?"
**Behavior:** Loads mechanic-brief. Quotes the approved Cosmology passage, states a design intent ("encourage rationing powerful casts; discourage spamming one"), lists decisions (what accumulates, decay vs. rest, hard cap vs. rising risk, visible vs. hidden), gives 2–3 options each, teaches *positive and negative feedback loops* where the decay choice changes whether strain snowballs, flags the dominant strategy "burst, then rest in a safe room" and proposes a counter, marks server needs. Writes a `draft` page; asks Brian to decide D1.

**Prompt:** "Explain the game theory behind faction reputation. I don't get why it matters."
**Behavior:** Anchors on the faction canon, then explains *incentive compatibility* and *dominant strategy* using a concrete reputation case (does helping two rival factions pay best?), and ends with the decision that changes.

## Stops
**Prompt:** "Make a mechanic for the Fifth's rift tolls." (page is `proposed`, not approved)
**Behavior:** Doesn't brief. Says canon isn't approved, routes the open question to world-builder.

## Non-triggers
- "Write the server requirements for strain" → mechanic-spec (once decisions are made).
- "Is 5 strain per cast too high?" → balance-model.

# Feature brief template

```markdown
# FEAT-NN — <name players would use>
Status: proposed            # proposed | planned | shipped | cut
Release: R<n> (must | cut-first)

## Problem
Who, and what they can't do today. Cite the demo or board that shows it.

## Outcome
What the player can do afterwards, in their words.

## Success signal
Observable check, how it's run, and by whom.
e.g. "In the R1 demo, a new player creates a Character and reaches the
commons in under 5 minutes without reading docs."

## Scope
- In: …
- Out: … (why)

## Dependencies
| Need | Owner | State |
|---|---|---|
| Combat rules | game-designer | `docs/mechanics/combat.md` proposed |
| Raider templates | producer | needs a C<n> story |
| Damage events in protocol v1 | architecture | open question #NNN |

## Decisions
- <call> — <one-line reason>

## Open for Brian
- <question> — recommendation: <option>, because …

## Sources
Product issues, demos, Notion pages, specs this brief draws on.

## Log
- YYYY-MM-DD: proposed
```

A brief is one page. If the Scope list needs sub-headings, it's two features.
